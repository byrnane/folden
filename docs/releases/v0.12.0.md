# Folden 0.12.0 beta

English · [Русский](#русский)

Folden is a desktop Markdown editor with visual and source editing, project folders, recovery, search, local images, templates, and printing.

**The beta may contain bugs, crash, or lose data.** Keep separate backups of important documents. Folden is provided “AS IS”, without warranties under the [usage terms](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/v0.12.0/LICENSE.md#warranty-and-liability).

## What's new

- Original app design, English and Russian documentation, and About Folden with version, licenses, and project links.
- File paths and operations adapted for Windows, macOS, and Linux, including older saved paths.
- Command shortcuts and system printing on macOS.
- Official installers for Windows x64, macOS Intel, and macOS Apple Silicon.
- Checks of package versions, architectures, licenses, and privacy, with SHA-256 checksums.

## Download and install

[Download beta 0.12.0](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/releases/tag/v0.12.0). Choose your system's package and compare its SHA-256 with the matching entry in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The release includes license texts and the NSIS source archive required by the Windows installer's license.

Windows requires WebView2. macOS requires version 14 or newer. Windows packages are unsigned; macOS packages have an ad-hoc signature and are not notarized. Your system may warn or block the first launch. See the [installation instructions](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/master/README.md#installation) and [test results](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/master/docs/BETA-0.12.0.md).

Linux has no official package or recorded testing on a real system for this beta. You may [build the unmodified source for your own use](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/v0.12.0/docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#linux-self-build).

Official builds are free for personal and commercial work. Source inspection and private Linux builds are permitted under [LICENSE.md](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/v0.12.0/LICENSE.md); other reuse, modification, and redistribution are restricted. Your documents belong to you. Third-party components retain their own licenses.

### Загрузка и установка

[Скачать бету 0.12.0](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/releases/tag/v0.12.0). Выберите пакет для своей системы и сравните его SHA-256 с записью в `SHA256SUMS.txt`. В выпуск входят тексты лицензий и архив исходников NSIS, обязательный по лицензии Windows-установщика.

Windows требует WebView2, macOS — версии 14 и новее. Windows-пакеты не подписаны; macOS-пакеты имеют ad-hoc подпись и не прошли нотариальное заверение Apple. Система может предупредить или заблокировать первый запуск. Подробнее — в [инструкции установки](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/master/README.ru.md#установка) и [результатах проверок](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/master/docs/BETA-0.12.0.ru.md).

Официального Linux-пакета и записанных проверок этой беты на реальной системе нет. Можно [собрать неизменённые исходники для собственного использования](https://github.com/byrnane/folden/blob/v0.12.0/docs/DEVELOPMENT.ru.md#самостоятельная-сборка-на-linux).
